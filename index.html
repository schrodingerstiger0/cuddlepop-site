The 4-Stage Protocol

How a fight works.

A fight is its own workspace: a shared thread, a private thread for each of you, a plan, and a repair score that reflects what actually happens afterwards.

1 Phase: Open

Each of you tells Oliver privately

Oliver asks a few questions each and listens for what you need, not just what happened. No rush, no interruptions.

record_voice_over Safe vent zone
2 Phase: Cooling

A summary you both call fair

Oliver reflects both sides back in the shared thread. Either of you can say “not quite” and it revises until you agree.

sync_alt 3 revisions max
3 Phase: Resolving

A plan with real action items

One to three things per person, time-bound, with a check-in date. Both accept before it counts into the relationship ledger.

task_alt Mutual acceptance
4 Phase: Resolved

Follow-through, not just good intentions

Journals, stories and check-ins feed an effort trend. Your partner sees the trend arrow, never your private entries.

trending_up Repair score moves up
sports_score Live in-person or on phone

Let's Fight: a referee for the real thing.

Tap Let's Fight when an argument is actually happening. Oliver listens, knows who is speaking, and stays silent until one of you says “Hey Oliver”.

  • check_box Both of you consent on your own phones before anything is recorded.
  • check_box Oliver answers in a sentence or two: who said what, where the misunderstanding is, and whether a timeout would help.
  • check_box No diagnosis, no taking sides. If it is not sure who spoke, it asks.
  • check_box When you stop, Oliver writes the fight record and opens a fight with the summary and draft plan.
  • check_box Audio is deleted immediately after transcription. The transcript stays in the fight vault.

Zero-Leak Engineering Pledge

“If the wall fails once, the product is dead.”

That line is in our engineering plan, and it is why the wall is three separate layers rather than a promise in a system prompt. Oliver can use what it knows privately to decide how to advise. It can never use it to decide what to reveal.

  • verified Private chats and journals are never quoted, paraphrased, or hinted at in shared threads.
  • verified Anything shared from a private conversation needs your explicit yes, each time.
  • verified Let's Fight audio is deleted after transcription.
  • verified Ending the relationship locks the data for both of you. Either can ask to recover, and the other has to accept.
  • verified If something looks like crisis or abuse, Oliver drops the both-sides framing with you privately and points to real help.
Layer 01

Data layer

Every memory, journal entry and game answer carries a privacy scope. The shared thread is built from shared rows only. Your partner's private rows cannot be loaded into your thread.

Layer 02

Prompt layer

In shared threads Oliver is told it has no access to private conversations and must never imply it does. In private threads it is told nothing said there is shared.

Layer 03

Guard layer

Every message bound for a shared thread is checked against the other person's private memories. A leak means a rewrite, then a safe fallback, and an incident logged.

Clear Answers

Questions couples ask first.

Is this therapy? keyboard_arrow_down
No. Cuddle Pop is not a therapist, does not diagnose, and says so plainly whenever a conversation gets serious. If it detects signs of crisis or abuse, it stops mediating and points you to real resources for your region.
Can my partner see what I say in private? keyboard_arrow_down
No. Private chats, journal entries and private game answers never appear in shared threads, in any form. The only thing your partner can see from your journal is an effort trend, and only if you turn that on.
What if Oliver takes my partner's side? keyboard_arrow_down
In shared threads Oliver only says what both of you have confirmed is fair. You can push back on any summary and it will revise, up to three rounds, before anything moves forward.
Does Let's Fight record us all the time? keyboard_arrow_down
No. Recording starts only after both of you consent, and only for that session. Audio is deleted once it has been transcribed. You can end the session at any moment.
What happens if we break up? keyboard_arrow_down
Either of you can end the relationship. Both of you lose live access and the data is locked. Either can later propose recovery, and the other has to accept. You can ask for a permanent delete at any time.
Does it work long-distance? keyboard_arrow_down
It was designed for that. Both of you reach the same Oliver from anywhere, the shared space works across time zones, and Let's Fight works when you are in the same room or on a call.
