The Zero-Leak Promise

"If the wall fails once, the product is dead."

That line is in our engineering plan. The privacy wall is 3 separate physical layers, not a wishful system prompt.

Data Layer Segregation

Every memory, journal entry and game answer has a privacy scope. Your partner's private rows literally can't load into your thread.

LAYER 02 Context Blindness
Prompt Layer Walls

In shared threads Oliver has zero access to private chats and can't imply it does. In private threads, nothing gets shared.

LAYER 03 Incident Tripwires
Guard Layer Interceptors

Every message headed for a shared thread is checked against private memories. A leak triggers an instant rewrite and fallback.
