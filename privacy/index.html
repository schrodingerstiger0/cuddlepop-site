Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

The short version. What you tell Oliver in private stays private. Your partner can't see your private chats or your journal, and nothing you say privately is shared unless you say so. Your data is encrypted at rest and stored in Australia. None of it is used to train AI. You can download it or delete it from the app.

Who we are

Cuddle Pop is an app with Oliver, the friend a couple shares. If you use Cuddle Pop on your own, your best friend in the app plays that part for you. Oliver is an AI, not a person, and Cuddle Pop is not therapy.

Cuddle Pop is run by Neurosapiens Pvt Ltd, New Delhi, India. In this policy "we" means that entity. For privacy questions, write to nihal@cuddlepop.com.

This policy covers the Cuddle Pop app, the invite page a partner opens from an invite link, and the website at cuddlepop.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address. You sign in with a one-time code we email to you. There are no passwords.
  • The name you give us, your age, and any other profile details you choose to add.
  • Cuddle Pop is only for people aged 18 and over, so we ask for your age before you can use it.

What you write and share

  • Your chats with Oliver and with your best friend in the app, your shared chat with your partner, your journal, your side of a fight, plans and dates, and game answers.
  • Photos, videos and voice notes you send.
  • Let's Fight audio, only when you both opt in. The audio is deleted once it has been transcribed.

Your device

  • A push notification token, so we can send the notifications you have turned on. You can change notification settings in the app.

If someone invites you

If your partner invites you, you can tell Oliver your side on the invite page without an account. We keep the name and details you give and what you write. It is not shared with your partner unless you say yes, and you can delete the whole thing at any time from the same page.

On our website

  • The marketing pages at cuddlepop.com use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and clicks. It runs only on cuddlepop.com's marketing pages, not in the app, not on the invite page and not on this page.
  • If you join the early-access list, we keep your email address to tell you when the app is ready. The list lives in our database in Sydney, Australia, encrypted at rest. With the address we keep the date you joined, which page you joined from and any referral tag in the link you used; nothing else. It is not deleted automatically: we keep it until we have told you the app is ready, then delete it. To come off it sooner, write to nihal@cuddlepop.com.

How we use it

  • To run Cuddle Pop: sign you in, keep your chats, and let Oliver reply.
  • To keep private things private. The shared chat can't open your private chats or your journal, so Oliver has nothing from them to quote or hint at. Oliver checks every shared message for leaks and rewrites anything that slips.
  • To keep you safe. If Oliver spots signs of a crisis or abuse, Oliver stops and points you to real help.
  • To improve the website, using the analytics described above.

We do not use what you tell Oliver to train AI, and we do not sell your data. There is no advertising in Cuddle Pop, and we do not share your data with advertisers.

Who else processes it

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the app's servers, database and media storage in the Sydney, Australia region (ap-southeast-2).
  • Anthropic provides the AI model behind Oliver. To reply, Oliver sends the relevant messages to Anthropic over an encrypted connection.
  • Resend sends the sign-in code emails.
  • Google provides the website analytics.
  • The photos and screenshots you send also go to Anthropic, so Oliver can see what you shared.
  • Expo, Apple and Google deliver push notifications. We hand each notification to Expo's push service, which passes it to Apple or Google for your phone. They see your push token and the text of the notification.
  • Built in but not switched on yet, listed here so you know before they are: Deepgram would turn Let's Fight audio and voice notes into text; Voyage AI would turn Oliver's memories into search vectors; Sentry would receive crash reports with no message text, e-mail address or IP address; and RevenueCat, with Apple and Google, would handle Plus billing once Plus is on sale. We will update the date at the top of this policy when any of them is turned on.

Our servers and your stored data are in Australia. Anthropic, Resend, Expo, Apple and Google process data outside Australia, for example in the United States. Everything we send them travels over an encrypted connection, and we send only what the job needs. Anthropic gets what Oliver works on: the chat messages Oliver replies to, the memories Oliver draws on, what you write in your journal and in Let's Fight, the photos and screenshots you share, and the game, date and plan content Oliver helps with. Resend gets the e-mail address a sign-in code goes to. Expo, Apple and Google get your push token and the notification text. Google Analytics on the website processes visit data outside Australia too.

How we protect it

  • Your chats, journal, fight sides and summaries, plans, memories, captions, game answers, names and email address are encrypted at rest, with keys per person or per relationship.
  • Photos, videos and voice notes are encrypted before they are stored, and every time one is opened the read is logged.
  • You can turn on an app lock in the app, so Cuddle Pop asks for Face ID or your fingerprint.

This is encryption at rest, not end-to-end encryption. Oliver needs to read what you write in order to reply, so our servers can decrypt it. When your data is purged we delete the keys too, so copies left in old backups can no longer be read.

Your partner and your privacy

  • Your partner can't see your private chats or your journal. If you both opt in, your partner sees only your effort trend.
  • Nothing you tell Oliver privately is shared unless you say so.
  • When you invite someone, they get a link and one line. Nothing you told Oliver goes with it.
  • If you break up, either of you can lock Cuddle Pop for both of you, and we wipe the data on request.

Your choices

  • Download your data. In the app you can download a copy of your data.
  • Delete your account. Deleting your account deletes your data and destroys its encryption keys.
  • Voice notes. You choose how long voice notes are kept: 30 days, 90 days or forever.
  • Notifications. You choose which notifications you get.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, export it, delete it, or withdraw a consent you gave. You can download a copy of your data and delete your account in the app, as described above. For anything else, write to nihal@cuddlepop.com. We answer every request within 30 days.

  • India. You have these rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. If you have a complaint, write to nihal@cuddlepop.com first. If we don't resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • European Economic Area and United Kingdom. Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can also object to how we use your data or ask us to restrict it. You can complain to the data protection supervisory authority where you live.
  • Australia. You have these rights under the Privacy Act 1988. If you are not happy with how we handle a complaint, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

How long we keep it

  • Let's Fight audio: deleted once transcribed.
  • Voice notes: as long as your voice-note setting says.
  • Deleted photos, videos and voice notes: removed from storage about a day after deletion.
  • Your account and what you write: kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your private chats, journal, photos, voice notes, game answers, plans and memories are deleted straight away, your e-mail address, name and age are removed, your encryption key is destroyed, and anything you wrote in a shared chat is blanked out.
  • Ended relationships: when a couple ends their space, or one person leaves or deletes their account, the shared space becomes read-only for 180 days. Nobody new can join it. After 180 days its chats, photos, memories, fight summaries and plans, date plans and game answers are deleted (a fight keeps only its dates and status) and its encryption key is destroyed.
  • Sign-in codes: each code expires after 10 minutes. Used and expired codes are deleted the next time a code is issued, and all of yours are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Notification log: 30 days, or straight away when you delete your account.
  • Database backups: a daily encrypted backup of the database is kept for up to 37 days, plus a nightly snapshot of the server disk of which the last seven are kept. Your private content in a backup can no longer be read, because your encryption key is destroyed when you delete your account. What you wrote in a shared chat is blanked in the live database; its copies in backups stay readable to us until those backups expire, within about five weeks. Your e-mail address and name leave the backups when those backups expire, within about five weeks.

Children

Cuddle Pop is only for people aged 18 and over. If you think someone under 18 is using it, write to nihal@cuddlepop.com.
